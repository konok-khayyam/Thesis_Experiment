Purpose of the study

This study investigates whether people recognise different normative orientations expressed by a conversational AI system and whether interacting with a chatbot communicating in a particular way is associated with participants’ subsequent choices in new social situations. The study considers communication that may emphasise personal independence, competition and achievement, cooperation and shared well-being, or obligations toward groups.

What participation involves

You will provide consent, answer demographic and pre-chat questionnaire items, interact with an AI chatbot for approximately five minutes, answer questions about the chatbot and choose responses to four social scenarios. Estimated total duration: [insert approved duration].

About the AI chatbot

The chatbot generates responses automatically using an AI model. It will be assigned one of several communication orientations as part of the research design. The assigned orientation will not be disclosed during the interaction.
