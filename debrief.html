What was this study about?

This study investigates whether people recognise different normative orientations expressed by conversational AI and whether interacting with a chatbot communicating in a particular way is associated with their subsequent choices in social situations.

The chatbot was assigned one of five communication orientations: horizontal individualism, vertical individualism, horizontal collectivism, vertical collectivism, or neutral. These orientations differ in their emphasis on independence, competition, cooperation and group obligations. Participants were assigned to one condition to enable comparisons across conditions.
